Cauli Flower

with Stim Rice
Preparation: 10 minutes
Cooking: 15 minutes
Total: 25 minutes
Servings: 2 persons
Ingredients
    🥣 For the Steamed Rice:
  • 1 cup basmati rice (or any long-grain white rice)
  • 2 cups water
  • 1/2 tsp oil or ghee
  • A pinch of salt
    • 🥣 For the Cauliflower (Gobi Masala):
  • 1 medium head cauliflower, cut into bite-sized florets
  • 1 medium onion, finely chopped
  • 1 medium tomato, finely chopped
  • 1 tsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 2 tbsp cooking oil
  • 1/2 tsp cumin seeds
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp red chili powder (or Kashmiri chili for mild heat)
  • 1 tsp coriander-cumin powder
  • 1/2 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 cup water
  • Fresh coriander leaves, chopped (for garnish)
Directions
1.Cook the Steamed Rice:

Rinse the rice thoroughly under cold running water 2–3 times until the water runs clear.

Add the rinsed rice, 2 cups of water, a pinch of salt, and oil to a pot.

Bring to a boil, then cover with a tight-fitting lid, lower the heat to low, and simmer for 10–12 minutes until the water is fully absorbed. Turn off the heat and let it rest covered for 5 minutes before fluffing with a fork.

2.Prep the Cauliflower:

Soak the cut cauliflower florets in warm salted water for 5 minutes, then drain completely. This cleans the florets and removes any residual dirt.

3.Make the Masala Base:

Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a pan or skillet over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds.

Add the chopped onions and sauté for 2–3 minutes until soft and translucent.

Stir in the ginger-garlic paste and cook for 30 seconds until the raw aroma disappears.

Add the chopped tomatoes, turmeric, red chili powder, coriander-cumin powder, and salt. Cook for 2–3 minutes until the tomatoes soften and blend into a thick masala paste.

4. Cook the Cauliflower:

Add the drained cauliflower florets to the pan. Toss well so the spices coat every piece.

Pour in 1/2 cup of water. Cover with a lid and cook on low-medium heat for 7–8 minutes, stirring once halfway through, until the cauliflower is fork-tender but not mushy.

Uncover, sprinkle the garam masala, and toss gently. Turn off the heat and garnish with fresh coriander leaves.

5.Plate and Serve:

Ladle a warm serving of fluffy steamed rice into a bowl and top with the cauliflower curry. Serve hot.

Quick Pro Tips

Don't Overcook: Cauliflower turns mushy quickly; check with a fork at the 7-minute mark. It should be tender with a slight bite.

One-Pot Shortcut: To save time and pots, add the washed rice and raw cauliflower florets directly into the sauteed masala base with 2 cups of water, cover, and pressure-cook for 2 whistles to make a quick Cauliflower Pulao.

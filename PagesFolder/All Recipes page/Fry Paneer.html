Air Fry Paneer Tikka Salad

Preparation: 15 minutes
Cooking: 10 minutes
Total: 25 minutes
Servings: 2 persons
Ingredients
    🥣 For the Air Fryer Paneer Tikka:
  • 200g firm paneer, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • 1/2 cup thick hung curd (Greek yogurt)
  • 1 tbsp ginger-garlic paste
  • 1 tbsp mustard oil (or regular olive oil)
  • 1 tsp Kashmiri red chili powder (for color and mild heat)
  • 1/2 tsp turmeric powder
  • 1 tsp roasted cumin powder
  • 1 tsp garam masala
  • 1/2 tsp chaat masala
  • 1 tsp kasuri methi (crushed dried fenugreek leaves)
  • 1 tbsp lemon juice
  • 1/2 tsp salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1/2 medium red bell pepper, cubed (optional)
  • 1/2 medium red onion, cubed (optional)
    • 🥣 For the Salad Base:
  • 3 cups mixed salad greens (romaine, baby spinach, or lettuce), washed and torn
  • 1/2 cup English cucumber, diced or sliced into half-moons
  • 1/2 cup cherry tomatoes, halved
  • 1/4 cup thinly sliced red onion rings
    • 🥣 For the Mint-Yogurt Dressing:
  • 1/3 cup plain Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp fresh mint-coriander chutney
  • 1 tbsp fresh lemon juice
  • 1/4 tsp chaat masala
  • Salt and black pepper, to taste
  • 1–2 tsp water (to thin if needed)
Directions
1.Marinate the Paneer:

In a mixing bowl, whisk together the hung curd, ginger-garlic paste, mustard oil, Kashmiri red chili powder, turmeric, cumin powder, garam masala, chaat masala, crushed kasuri methi, lemon juice, and salt.

Add the paneer cubes (along with the cubed peppers and onions, if using). Gently fold with a spatula until every piece is well coated. Let it rest for 10–15 minutes.

2. Air Fry the Paneer:

Preheat the air fryer to 190°C (375°F) for 3 minutes.

Lightly grease the air fryer basket with oil spray. Arrange the marinated paneer cubes in a single layer, ensuring they do not touch.

Air fry at 190°C for 8 to 10 minutes, shaking the basket halfway through, until the edges are nicely charred and golden brown. Avoid overcooking to keep the paneer soft.

3.Whisk the Dressing:

In a small bowl, whisk the Greek yogurt, mint chutney, lemon juice, chaat masala, salt, and pepper together until silky. Add a splash of water if you prefer a pourable consistency.

4.Assemble the Salad:

In a large salad bowl, arrange the mixed greens, cucumbers, cherry tomatoes, and red onion rings.

Top with the warm air-fried paneer cubes.

Drizzle the mint-yogurt dressing generously over the top, sprinkle a pinch of chaat masala, and serve immediately.

Pro Tips for Best Results

Avoid Rubber Paneer: Overcooking paneer pulls out moisture and turns it chewy. 8–10 minutes in a hot air fryer is plenty to get blistered edges while keeping the center tender.

Keep Greens Crisp: Assemble the salad and drizzle the dressing just before serving so the heat from the paneer doesn't wilt the crisp greens.

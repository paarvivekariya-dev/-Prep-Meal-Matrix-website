Veg Sandwich

with Row vegis
Preparation: 10 minutes
Cooking: 3-5 minutes
Total: 18 minutes
Servings: 2 persons
Ingredients
    For the Zucchini Noodles (Zoodles):
  • 3 medium fresh zucchinis, trimmed
  • 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
    • For the Avocado Pesto Sauce:
  • 1 ripe avocado, pitted and peeled
  • 1 cup fresh basil leaves, packed
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 2 tbsp lemon juice (freshly squeezed)
  • 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
  • 3 tbsp walnuts, almonds, or pine nuts
  • 2 tbsp grated Parmesan cheese (use nutritional yeast for a 100% vegan option)
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt (adjust to taste)
  • 1/4 tsp cracked black pepper
  • 1–2 tbsp water (optional, to thin the sauce if needed)
    • Optional Garnish:
  • Halved cherry tomatoes
  • Toasted pine nuts or crushed red chili flakes
  • Fresh basil leaves
Directions
1.Prepare the Zucchini Noodles: Spiralize the zucchinis using a spiralizer or a julienne peeler to form long noodle-like strands. Transfer the zoodles to a plate lined with paper towels and gently pat dry to remove surface moisture.
2. Blend the Avocado Pesto: In a food processor or high-speed blender, combine the avocado flesh, fresh basil leaves, garlic cloves, nuts, lemon juice, olive oil, salt, and black pepper. Blend on high until completely smooth and creamy. If the pesto is too thick, stream in 1 to 2 tablespoons of cold water until it reaches a smooth sauce consistency. Stir in the Parmesan cheese.
3.Sauté the Zoodles: Heat 1 tablespoon of olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Add the zucchini noodles and toss quickly for 2 to 3 minutes just until tender-crisp. Do not overcook, or the zucchini will release excess moisture and turn soggy.
4. Combine and Serve: Remove the skillet from heat completely. Pour the avocado pesto over the warm noodles and toss gently with tongs until evenly coated. Plate immediately and garnish with halved cherry tomatoes, fresh basil, and chili flakes.
Pro Tips for Website Readers. Avoid Soggy Noodles: Salt draws water out of zucchini. Avoid salting the zoodles while they cook; instead, season the pesto sauce properly and dress the dish right before serving. Serve Immediately: Because fresh avocado oxidizes and turns brown when exposed to air, this dish is best served immediately after tossing.
